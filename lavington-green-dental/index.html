Our services

Dental care, tailored to you

Emergency and scheduled visits across seven areas of care, from prevention to full smile restoration.

General Dental Treatment

Extractions, fillings, root canal treatment, crowns and bridges.

Cosmetic Dentistry

Treatment focused on the appearance and confidence of your smile.

Paediatric Dentistry

Gentle, age-appropriate care for children, from fillings to space maintainers.

Orthodontic Treatment

Aligning and repositioning teeth using braces or retainers.

Prosthetic Dentistry

Dentures, dental implants, crowns and bridges.

Minor Oral Surgery

A practice built on trust, upgraded every year

Lavington Green Dental Suite (formerly Lavington Dental Suite) has spent over a decade offering comprehensive, affordable care. The facility is regularly upgraded, well-equipped dental units, an intraoral digital x-ray machine, a sterilization unit, and a comfortable reception with a patient education corner.

Beyond the clinic, our team works with surrounding schools and community members on oral health awareness, because a healthy smile shouldn't be a privilege.

Yes, we work with 20+ insurance providers and a number of corporate accounts. See our insurance & corporate page for the full list.

Yes, our paediatric dentistry covers fillings, extractions, and preventive care for younger patients.

Treatment is available on both emergency and scheduled visits, message us on WhatsApp and we'll guide you.

Do I need an appointment? +

Walk-ins are welcome, though booking ahead on WhatsApp or by phone helps us minimise your wait.
