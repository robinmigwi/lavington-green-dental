A decade and a half of caring for Lavington's smiles

A Dental Surgery Consultancy, Research and Management organization incorporated in the Republic of Kenya, previously known as Lavington Dental Suite.

Our history

Operating since 2011

Lavington Green Dental Suite offers a wide range of dental services at affordable costs. Our services are comprehensive, specialized and tailored to meet the needs of our clients.

Beyond the chair, we specialize in creating awareness about common oral health problems in collaboration with the immediate community, including surrounding schools and less fortunate members of our society.

2011

Founded as Lavington Dental Suite, offering general dentistry to the Lavington community.

Ongoing

Regular renovation and equipment upgrades, including an intraoral digital x ray machine and modern dental units.

Today

Operating as Lavington Green Dental Suite, serving individual, insured and corporate clients across Nairobi.

Your visit

What you can expect when you visit.

We want every patient to understand what is happening and feel supported throughout their care.

A conversation first

Tell the team what is worrying you or what you would like help with, in your own words.

Clear explanations

A shared next step

Inside our facility
Chandarana Building, 3rd Floor, Lavington Green Shopping Centre

Our facility is fitted with well equipped dental units, an intraoral digital x ray machine, a spacious and comfortable reception area with a patient education corner, a sterilization unit, a private doctor's office for consultations, and a staff room. We continue to upgrade regularly to keep pace with new technology and our clients' needs.
